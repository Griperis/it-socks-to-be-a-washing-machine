# Batch C review sheet

What the designer plays at the end of batch C (the lived-in house: the day and the exterior, the Elevator, the Cat and
the litterbox, the X-rays, the vacuums, the detector, the TV and the Radio; Q2614), in one session (Q2605), and what the
agents settled on their own along the way (Q2604). The build order: `docs/migration/milestones.md` "Batch C". The
designs: `docs/architecture/elevator.md`, `day-and-exterior.md`, `devices.md`, `tv-and-radio.md`, `cat.md`,
`x-rays.md`.

## How to use it
Play a house New Game from the title, Solo first, then with a Guest, at least until the ground floor opens (the Cat
leaves the couch, the vacuums are reachable) and on until night (19:30: every lamp on; the debug speed of the day, if
the build has one, shortens the wait). F9 gives the Host 1000 Coins: buy Elevator Repair, the vacuum Upgrades and both
X-rays. Then visit the zoo's batch C stations (x = 208 to 292). For each line say yes, or what to change; a change
becomes a ticket. The asset line-ups are in `builds/review-<family>/`, the X-ray stills in `builds/review-xrays/`.
