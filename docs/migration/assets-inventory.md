# It Socks: asset inventory for the port

Read-only survey of `/c/Users/Gripe/personal/repositories/it-socks` (branch main, 2026-11-19). Nothing was run except `ls`, `find`, `grep`, `python3 tools/layout.py` and one-off read-only Python that parses the files. Helper scripts I wrote live only in the scratchpad (`glbscan.py`, `orig.py`).

How numbers were measured, in general: file counts and sizes by walking the folders (`os.walk`, `os.path.getsize`, `.import`, `.asd` and `.uid` files left out of the "files" counts, sizes are bytes on disk, MB = 1,048,576). glb contents (nodes, materials, images, triangles, scale, collision nodes) by reading each glb's JSON chunk. Triangles are counted from the index accessors of visible (non-collision) mesh nodes.

## 2. Furniture

Measured with `python3 tools/layout.py pieces|kinds` and by parsing `assets/house/furniture/manifest.json` and `house_layout.json`.

- Pieces: 67 (manifest `pieces`, 67 glb).
- Placements: 73 in `house_layout.json` `placements[]`: ground 23, first 22, basement 15, grotto 7, attic 6. They use 61 distinct pieces. 6 pieces are never placed: armchair, bikes, drying_rack, hand_basin, sheeted_sofa, side_table (`layout.py pieces` "placed 0").
- Pieces with moving parts / containers: 19 (the manifest `parts` and `containers` keys are both on exactly those 19). 28 containers, 119 parts: 107 hinge, 12 slide. By type: 88 piano keys (`Key_21`..`Key_108`), 12 `Drawer_<n>`, 11 `Door` (incl. `Door_Top`, `Door_Bottom` on the fridge, `Door_L/Door_R`), 6 `Lid`, 1 `Flap` (litterbox), 1 `Boot` (car). Container kinds in the house (`layout.py kinds`): cupboard 9 pieces/16 openables, drawer 6/12, fridge 1/2, oven 1/1, basket 1/1, litter_flap 1/1, piano_lid 1/1, plus the Grotto hatch.
- How moving parts are named in the glb: each piece root holds `Body` plus CapitalCase nodes `Door`, `Door_L`, `Door_R`, `Door_<n>`, `Drawer_<n>`, `Lid`, `Flap`, `Boot`, `Key_<midi>`. The node origin is the pivot (hinge on its axis, drawer at its closed position). The glb does not carry the axis, the opening angle or distance, or the part's collision boxes: those are only in the manifest (`parts{pivot, axis, open_m or open_deg, kind, boxes}`). Documented in `fkit.py` docstring (lines 1-30) and the manifest `_doc`.
- Origin and axes: manifest `_doc` says origin is the floor centre of the bounding box, +z front. I checked all 67 glbs: min y is 0.00 and x and z are centred for all of them (no exceptions in the printed table). Wall-hung pieces (kitchen_wall_cupboard, wall_mirror, kitchen_shelf, towel_rail) are modelled at the floor and lifted by the placement's `y` (for example `y: 1.45`).
- Sock collision: stored in the glb. Every piece has collision-only nodes `sock_<n>-convcolonly` (913 nodes: 415 top level, 498 under moving parts, written as `sock_N-convcolonly.N`) or `sock_<n>-colonly` (4 trimeshes), made by `house_furniture/sockcol.py` at build. Largest: car 59, old_cot 53, bunk_bed 42. At load `HouseParts.build_model()` (`game/house/house_parts.gd:427`) takes them out and `SockColliders` puts them in one static body.
- Player/crawl collision: stored in the manifest. `colliders` (boxes, 206 in total) exist for 33 of the 67 pieces; the other 34 have no `colliders` key and the game uses one box over the footprint from `crawl_gap` up (manifest `_doc`). `HouseParts.assemble()` (house_parts.gd:78) copies them into the layout, so the layout's 692 `colliders[]` and the manifest both matter.
- Sock spots (resting tops): computed at build time and stored in the manifest as `spots` `[i, k, y, clear, seen]`, 53,260 entries in all (this is why the manifest is 1.16 MB; every piece has them except `telescope`, which has 0). Found on the drawn mesh by `house_furniture/tops.py` (148 lines), run by `build.py`. The game filters them against what the house leaves clear and joins them into `surfaces[]` in `HouseParts.tops()` (house_parts.gd:245). The manifest's `tops` (19 pieces) is described by its own `_doc` as "hand-written, unused".
- Containers (where Socks hide): `containers[{name, parts, kind, interior box, shelves_y}]` in the manifest only. Crawl space: `crawl_gap` (26 pieces), `crawl_box` (2). Hiding spots: `hide` (26 pieces). Lamp: only `old_tree` has one (`lamp`).
- Hold points: not on furniture. Where carried things sit in the hands is `data/tuning/hold.json` (per held kind: frame, at, turn). In glbs, only the Hamper has hand grips (empties `grip_left`, `grip_right`, `pour_lip`, `socks_floor`) and the Avatar has `HoldPoint` and `HatPoint`.
- Materials: 425 material slots for the 67 pieces (average 6.3), 12 pieces have more than the contract's `max_materials` of 8 (old_tree 15, laundry_shelf 13, car 12, coat_nook 12, bookshelf 11, kids_shelf 11, ...).

## 3. House shell

make_house.py writes one glb, not one per floor: `assets/house/house.glb`. It cuts every mesh that spans levels at the middle of each floor slab and exports nodes named `<group>_<level>` (`Walls_basement`, `Glass_first`; `docs/art/house/pipeline.md` "The shell is parted by level"). Nodes in the glb (all at the root, no hierarchy): per level `Floor_`, `Ceiling_`, `Glass_`, `Walls_`, `Trim_`, `Windows_`, `Stairs_`, `Roof`; 21 `CeilingLight_<room>`; 8 `Door_<doorway>` leaves; `LockChain_<floor>`, `LockDoor_<floor>`, `LockPadlock_<floor>`; plus 1,003 collision-only nodes (Wall 428, Window 190, DoorFrame 93, Spindle 61, Step 42, Lamp 34, GarageDoor 26, Glass 22, Rafter 20, Floor 19, LockLeaf 19, ...). ## 5. Technical problems with evidence

1. Untracked `.import` and new files. `git status -uall` shows 145 untracked lines ending `.import`; 124 of them are real asset imports (the other 21 belong to `audio/voiceover2/`). They include 120 `assets/avatar/wardrobe/*.glb.import` (every wardrobe glb is untracked as an import), 17 cabinet glb imports, `encyclopedia_book`, `hamper`, `old_tree`, `ui/drawers.svg`. 8 cabinet front-art PNGs (drinks, fruit, machinery, math, nature, tech, transport, vegetables) are themselves untracked. 959 `.import` files are tracked in all (794 under `assets/`). The Godot project of the port should decide once whether `.import` files are tracked and the repo's mix of both is a bug source.
2. Dangling imports. `assets/avatar/socks.glb.import` (the glb is gone, `make_leg_socks.py:6` says so), `assets/sample_crate/sample_crate.glb.import` (folder holds only the import); in `audio/voiceover`: `cutscene_6_part_1/2.wav.import` and `.asd` and in `old_tree`: `tree_part_grotto_1/2.wav.import`, wavs missing.
3. Duplicate audio. `audio/voiceover2/` (21 wav, 29.4 MB, untracked) is byte-identical to `audio/voiceover/`: the 21 md5 sums match. `docs/llm/rogue-machine/issues/04-talking-washing-machine.md:47` says the designer removes `voiceover2/` themselves. Washer voice lines are 44.1 kHz stereo wav (30 MB) while the 161 Old Tree lines are 22.05 kHz mono.
4. Duplicate textures. `assets/house/house_flat_*.png` (4) are byte-identical to `assets/flat/flat_flat_*.png` (`cmp` same for all four). The same images are also embedded in `house.glb` (3 of them) and `flat.glb` (4), and the loose house pngs are never named in `game/`, `tests/`, `tools/`, `data/` (stem search). 17 `assets/cabinet/<collection>_*_front_art.png` are loose copies of images already embedded in each cabinet glb (image has a `bufferView`, no `uri`); none is referenced by name. `assets/guide/guide_card.png` and `guide_card_1.png` are two near copies (differ at byte 47).
5. Legacy flat not used by the game. `assets/flat/` (glb 2.1 MB, png 0.27 MB, `flat_layout.json` 36 KB) is referenced only by `tools/blender/make_flat.py`, `tools/move_files.py`, `tools/code_map.py` and `credits.json`; no `.gd` in `game/` or `tests/` loads it. `make_flat.py` is still imported by `make_house.py` as a library (so it cannot just be dropped). `assets/wardrobe_station/wardrobe_station_fabric_palette.png` (made by `make_wardrobe_station.py:228`) and `assets/laundry_bin/laundry_bin_*.png` are also unreferenced loose exports.
6. Stem-search of unreferenced assets. I searched `game`, `tests`, `tools`, `data`, `project.godot`, `studio_intro` and the layout/manifest/credits json for each asset's file stem. 214 asset files have no hit: 186 `assets/audio/synth/*.ogg` (they are chosen at run time through `game/audio/sound_bank.gd` and `candidates.json`, so not necessarily dead), the 17+4+4 loose pngs above, the font's `OFL.txt` and the wardrobe-station palette. This is a name search, not proof; dynamic paths (`res://assets/cabinet/` + id) are matched through their stem only when the stem appears elsewhere, so treat "unreferenced" as a hint.
7. Six furniture pieces are built and exported but never placed (armchair, bikes, drying_rack, hand_basin, sheeted_sofa, side_table: `layout.py pieces`).
8. Two naming conventions. Script-built parts (furniture, shell, flat) use CapitalCase nodes: `Door`, `Drawer_1`, `Door_WorkshopLobby`, `Lid`, `Key_60`. MCP assets use lower snake_case: `door_left`, `front_cover`, `hour_hand`, `slot_NN`, `grip_left`. Counted by parsing node names matching door/drawer/lid/flap/hatch/hinge/cover/boot: furniture 31 Capitalised vs 2 lower; house 16 vs 12; cabinet 17 lower; washing_machine 3 lower; encyclopedia_book 1 lower. Collision suffixes differ too: shell and furniture `<name>-convcolonly`, `sock_<n>-convcolonly.<n>`; gadgets `body-convcolonly`, `floor-convcolonly`.
9. Pivot and axis data live outside the glb for furniture and the shell (manifest `parts`, layout `room_doors` with `hinge`, `axis`, `open_deg`). A port that wants "a scene with named moving pieces" has to move these into scene or node data.
10. Origins are not uniform. Furniture: floor centre. Shell, Elevator, exterior, Grotto: house space (NW inner corner), flat root list (house.glb has 1,093 root nodes, elevator 14, exterior 4). Manifest `origin_xy`/`origin_z` in `assets/blender_assets`: 93 wearables have both false (authored in their attach point's frame), 18 furniture-class assets have `origin_xy` false (the Cabinets, the wardrobe station...), the clock hangs by its back centre, the rest are floor-centred. No node in any glb has a non-1 scale (0 scaled nodes in all 241 glbs), so scale is consistent: 1 unit = 1 m.
11. Over the contract. `max_materials` is 8 in `_classes.json`, yet 21 glbs exceed it: house 44, grotto 22, old_tree 15, laundry_shelf 13, car 12, coat_nook 12, bookshelf 11, kids_shelf 11, shelf_rack 9, nightstand 9, vanity 9, paint_tins 9 and others.
12. Duplicate materials. 1,235 material slots across all glbs use 656 distinct names. Same name, different values: `oak` appears in 39 glbs with 9 different (colour, roughness, metallic) combinations (colour 0.6/0.28/0.09 with roughness 0.45, 0.55 or 0.6), `brass` 30 glbs/4 variants, `trim` 22 glbs/18 variants, `dark` 24 glbs/19 variants, `red` 28/6, `white` 21/5, `steel` 17/5. There is no shared material library: every glb carries its own copy, so recolouring or restyling needs a pass over all of them. Pure signature duplicates across files (same colour, roughness, metal) are 1,235 - 812 = 423.
13. Large files. Biggest: 3 HDR sky 2k (6.7-6.9 MB each, 19.7 MB), `prints.png` 3072x4608 (3.2 MB; import `compress/mode=0`, lossless, so it is 56 MB of RGBA in memory before mipmaps), `house.glb` 3.95 MB, `exterior.glb` 2.68 MB, radio music 17 MB, voice 74 MB (30 + 44). Texture sizes elsewhere are small (cabinet art 1024x256, house floor textures 1024x1024).
14. Fonts/UI. No UI font file other than Patrick Hand; the rest of the UI uses the engine default (Open Sans). `credits.json` also names DejaVu Sans / Bitstream for the guide card captions: that font is not in the repo as a file (those captions are rendered into the png by `tools/guide_card.py`).
15. Collision uses `-convcolonly` through the Godot importer but then `HouseParts.build_model()` strips the nodes and rebuilds boxes (house_parts.gd:517-606), turning 8-corner hulls back into boxes. A port that keeps collision in the glb must decide whether to keep that step.
16. Stale numbers in docs. `docs/architecture/campaign-house.md` says "605 Designs"; `data/designs.json` has 1,005. `docs/art/pipeline_mcp.md` says no LFS (see section 1).
