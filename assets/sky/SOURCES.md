# Sky sources

The three HDRIs the sky blends by the hour (core/18, core/160; `game/look/day_sky.gdshader`), the old game's: the
day photo is the zoo's fixed noon and the house's day, the dusk and night photos the house's evening and night. Each
is CC0 (public domain dedication) from Poly Haven, unchanged at 2k (2048x1024, Radiance .hdr), copied from the old
game's `assets/sky/`.
